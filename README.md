# Alfa Truck Trust

Design a full-width website section for the desktop site of "ALFA TRUCK" —

a European distributor of LED lighting and electrical parts for trucks,

trailers and special vehicles.

SECTION: "Why ALFA TRUCK" — the trust/persuasion block of the page.

It must convince a visitor (who found the company via a scanned business

card) that these people are legitimate and worth contacting.

CONTENT — headline "Why ALFA TRUCK" plus four short reasons:

1. Original products — official distributor of leading European lighting brands

2. Professional consultation — we match the right lighting to your vehicle

3. Quality and safety — certified products, compliant with the ECE standard

4. Delivery across Europe — we ship to every EU country

AESTHETIC — this is NOT a retail/webshop look. It should feel like a

premium automotive brand film still crossed with an editorial magazine

spread: dark, moody, cinematic, confident. Near-black background.

Refined serif display headline, restrained sans-serif for the rest.

Generous negative space, asymmetry, strong typographic hierarchy.

A single hero visual — a truck or one dramatic vehicle lamp — that

melts into the dark background with no visible frame or box.

Understated, expensive, quiet. No bright primary colors, no

icons-in-circles, no gradient buttons, no stock-photo collage.

Pick the accent color, layout and composition yourself — surprise me.

Output: one polished desktop section mockup, 16:9.
